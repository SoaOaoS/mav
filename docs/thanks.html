Thank you.
Welcome to Mav Connect.

Your payment went through. Your licence key is on its way to your inbox — it usually takes a minute or two.

1

Find your key

Look for an email from Mav with a key that starts with MAV1. Nothing after a few minutes? Check your spam folder, then write to us.

2

Activate it

In Mav, open Settings → Plan, paste the key and press Activate. Your plan switches to Connect right away.

3

Keep your recovery key safe

Under Cloud backup, press Recovery key and save it in your password manager. Backups are encrypted with it — it is the only way to restore on a new machine, and nobody, including us, can recover it.

Not up to date? Run sudo mav update first. Questions: contact support.
