An assistant for everyday life

It answers when you ask — and gets things done when you don't.

Chat, the way you know it

A clean ChatGPT-style app on your computer and phone. Chats get a title on their own; type / for quick commands.

Helpers with a speciality

The Assistant calls in a Researcher, a Writer, a Planner or Money when it helps. You always see who's answering — and can pick.

It remembers you

Your city, your diet, your projects. Mav learns facts about you and recalls them — see and edit everything on the Memory page.

Routines

"Every morning, tell me if I need an umbrella." Say it in a chat and confirm. Each routine has its own chat to follow up in.

Keeps an eye on things

A web page, a product's price, a news topic. You get a notification only when something actually changes.

Comes to you

Reports and alerts arrive as push notifications and wait in For you on the home screen — tap Tell me more.

Any model, no terminal

Claude, GPT, Ollama on your own hardware, OpenRouter… Switch from Settings: paste a key, pick a model from the list.

Private by design

Self-hosted: chats, memory and routines stay on your machine. No account, no telemetry — use a local model and nothing leaves home.

Running in three steps

From a spare machine to your own assistant in minutes.

  1. 1

    Install

    One command on a home server, a mini PC or a VPS. It asks only for your model and installs the rest.

    curl -fsSL …/get.sh | sudo bash
  2. 2

    Pick your model

    Choose a provider with the arrow keys, paste your key — it is checked right away — and pick a model from its list.

    Anthropic · OpenAI · Ollama · …
  3. 3

    Talk, then let it work

    Open the app on your computer or phone. Chat, add a routine, tell it what to keep an eye on — and it starts coming to you.

    mav update · mav status · mav logs

Small, self-contained, yours

The opencode agent engine does the thinking, a background worker handles routines and alerts, Postgres keeps the memory, and the web app ties it together. Everything on your machine.

  • Three services, set up for you
  • Memory in Postgres, migrated automatically
  • HTTPS + push notifications included
  • Painless updates: mav update
