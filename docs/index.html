It doesn't just answer. It acts.

Three layers keep Mav useful without turning it into a nag: it notices, it decides, it does.

1 · It notices

Triggers that fire on their own

On a schedule — daily, weekly, monthly, or every few hours. On an event from another service (a new PR, a form, a payment). Or only when a condition is met.

  • Say it in a chat — English, French or Spanish
  • Start from a ready-made template
  • Snooze any routine without losing it
2 · It decides

Proactivity, not noise

Every alert is ranked. Mav only interrupts when it clears the bar you set, and collects the rest into one calm daily recap.

Critical — always Important Useful → recap FYI — kept, silent
QuietNormalChatty
3 · It does

Drafts and background work

When a reply is needed, Mav writes it for you to review. Long tasks run in the background and come back when they're done — the chat stays yours.

An assistant for everyday life

It answers when you ask — and gets things done when you don't.

A briefing every morning

The weather where you live, what happened overnight, what needs you today — one short message at the time you choose.

No surprise bills

See what every chat and routine costs, set a monthly budget, and let a cheap model handle the background work.

Locked to you

Protected by your password, on your network, with your keys. Your chats and memory never leave your machine.

Chat, the way you know it

A clean ChatGPT-style app on your computer and phone. Chats get a title on their own; type / for quick commands.

Helpers with a speciality

The Assistant calls in a Researcher, a Writer, a Planner or Money when it helps. You always see who's answering — and can pick.

It remembers you

Your city, your diet, your projects. Mav learns facts about you and recalls them — see and edit everything on the Memory page.

Routines that fit real life

"Every morning, tell me if I need an umbrella." Or monthly, or when an event fires. Each routine has its own chat to follow up in.

Keeps an eye on things

A web page, a product's price, a news topic. You get a notification only when something actually changes.

Comes to you

Reports and alerts arrive as push notifications and wait in For you on the home screen — tap Tell me more.

Any model, no terminal

Claude, GPT, Ollama on your own hardware, OpenRouter… Switch from Settings: paste a key, pick a model from the list.

Private by design

Self-hosted: chats, memory and routines stay on your machine. No account, no telemetry — use a local model and nothing leaves home.

Connections, ready to use

Read web pages, a web browser, Brave search, time zones, your files, Notion, Home Assistant, GitHub. Pick one, paste its key.

Running in three steps

From a spare machine to your own assistant in minutes.

  1. 1

    Install

    One command on a home server, a mini PC or a VPS running Debian, Ubuntu, Arch or Fedora. It asks only for your model.

    curl -fsSL …/get.sh | sudo bash
  2. 2

    Pick your model

    Choose a provider with the arrow keys, paste your key — it is checked right away — and pick a model from its list.

    Anthropic · OpenAI · Ollama · …
  3. 3

    Talk, then let it work

    Open the app on your computer or phone. Chat, add a routine, tell it what to keep an eye on — and it starts coming to you.

    mav update · mav status · mav logs

Small, self-contained, yours

The opencode agent engine does the thinking, a background worker handles routines, events and alerts, Postgres keeps the memory, and the web app ties it together. Everything on your machine.

  • Three services, set up for you
  • Memory in Postgres, migrated automatically
  • HTTPS + push notifications included
  • Painless updates: mav update
