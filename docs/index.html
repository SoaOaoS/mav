The film

Meet Mav in 46 seconds.

What it does, how it feels, and why it stays on your machine.

Proactive, not noisy

It doesn't just answer. It acts.

Three layers keep Mav useful without turning it into a nag.

01 — It notices

Triggers that fire on their own

On a schedule — daily, weekly, monthly or every few hours. On an event from another service. Or only when a condition is met.

  • Say it in a chat, in your own words
  • Or pick a ready-made idea in one tap
  • Snooze any routine without losing it
02 — It decides

Every alert is ranked

Mav only interrupts you when something clears the bar you set. Everything else waits in one calm daily recap.

  • Quiet, Normal or Chatty, per routine
  • Conditions like “only if it rains”
03 — It does

Drafts and background work

When a reply is needed, Mav writes it for you to review. Long tasks run in the background and come back when they're done. The chat stays yours.

  • Copy, send or discard a draft in one tap
  • Files land in the chat as download cards
