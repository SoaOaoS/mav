Questions

Is Mav still open source?

Yes — MIT, all of it, including the Pro code. Pro is how you support the project and lift the Free limits; it is not a different app.

What happens if my Pro licence expires?

Nothing switches off. Your routines and watches keep running; you just can't add new ones beyond the Free limits until you renew.

Does the licence phone home?

No. A licence key is a signed text that Mav checks on your machine. No account server, no tracking, works offline.

Do I still pay my AI provider?

Yes — Mav uses your own key (or a free local model with Ollama). The Usage tab shows what you spend and lets you set a monthly budget.
