# Mav brand — "Midnight & Emerald"

Mav is calm, precise and always on. It works while you sleep, then hands you
the few things that matter. The brand says exactly that: a quiet midnight
canvas, an emerald that means "Mav", and one warm amber signal for the
moments when Mav comes to you.

## The mark

Two flat shapes: an **emerald disc** and an **amber dot** that cuts a small
notch into it, like a notification. Mav, with something for you.

Geometry (in a 100 × 100 box): disc centre (44, 56), radius 44; dot centre
(82, 18), radius 16; the notch is a circle of radius 23 around the dot.
No gradients, no shadows.

| Asset | Where |

| Favicon / app mark (SVG) | `docs/assets/favicon.svg`, `dashboard/icons/favicon.svg` |
| Logo (mark + wordmark, light) | `docs/assets/logo.svg` |
| Logo (mark + wordmark, dark) | `docs/assets/logo-dark.svg` |
| App icons (PNG, PWA) | `dashboard/icons/*.png`, generated by `dashboard/tools/make_icons.py` |
| Live mark (CSS) | `.orb` / `.splash-orb` in both style sheets — the dot pulses while Mav thinks |

Rules: keep clear space of half the disc around it; never recolour the disc
or the dot; on photos, put it on a midnight tile (as the app icon does).
The wordmark is "Mav" in Geist SemiBold, tracked tight (−0.04 em), never all
caps.

## Colour

| Role | Light | Dark | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0E1013` | `#EEF0F2` | text, marketing CTAs |
| Ink 2 / 3 | `#4A4F57` / `#8A9099` | `#AAB1BA` / `#747C86` | secondary text, hints |
| Paper | `#FAFAF7` | `#0B0D10` (midnight) | page background |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | `#13161A` | cards, composer |
| Line | `#E6E5DF` | `#22272D` | hairlines |
| **Emerald** (brand) | `#0F7A5C` | `#34D39D` | app actions, links, the disc |
| Emerald soft | `#E4F2EC` | `#0F2A21` | selected states, notices |
| **Amber** (signal) | `#E8962E` | `#F5B04F` | proactive moments only: briefing, alerts |
| Danger | `#C2412D` | `#F0806D` | errors, destructive actions |

Two rules keep it calm:
1. **Amber is rare.** It marks Mav reaching out (the *Brief me* chip,
   the dot of the mark). If everything is amber, nothing is.
2. **Marketing CTAs are ink, app actions are emerald.** The site invites
   calmly; the app acts clearly.

## Type

| Family | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Instrument Serif** (400, *italic*) | Editorial moments only: hero and section titles, the app's greeting and view titles. Italic for the emphasised half of a headline. |
| **Geist** (400–700) | Everything else: the wordmark, UI, body, buttons, chat. |
| **Geist Mono** | Code, commands, numbers in tables. |

Headlines are tight (−0.02 to −0.025 em), body text slightly tightened
(−0.006 em). Serif is never bold.

## Shape and depth

- Radii: 18 / 12 / 9 / 6 px in the app (cards, controls, chips, tags); 20 /
  14 px on the site; pills are fully round.
- Depth comes from **hairline rings + soft, long shadows** (`--shadow`,
  `--shadow-lg`), not from borders. Glass on the site only.
- Motion: 120–200 ms, ease-out; the dot's pulse is the only looping animation.

## Voice

Short, warm, specific. Say what Mav did and what it needs ("2 drafts to
approve"), not what it is. No hype words, no exclamation marks except to
thank people.

## Where the tokens live
